Summer 2026 · Rocketry Camp

Liftoff Labs

Five days, a group of future engineers, and plenty of "wow"s.

← Back to all builds

I started a five-day summer camp for middle schoolers (the younger side) to get more kids involved in rocketry. I designed the curriculum, marketed the camp, and ran the whole thing. Throughout the week, the group learned basic physics and rocket anatomy, constructed + launched their own kits, and collaborated together to create a single 15-inch custom rocket.
